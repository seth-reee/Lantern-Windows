Lantern diagnostic build

Double-click Lantern-Diagnostics.cmd to run Lantern with detailed scanner logging.
Reproduce the slow or frozen scan, wait about 30 seconds after progress stops,
then close Lantern. The log is saved at:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\Lantern\diagnostics.log

The log records Windows and graphics settings, scan ranges and host IP addresses,
probe timings, errors, and whether the UI heartbeat continued. It does not record
reverse-DNS hostnames or packet contents. Review it for private network details
before sharing it.
